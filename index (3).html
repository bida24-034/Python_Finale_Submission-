Policy

Our Policy

Claiming Period
01

Claim Waiting Periods

Before your first claim, you must wait 6 months from the date your plan activates. After a successful claim, you must wait 3 calendar months before submitting another. Waiting periods begin from the date of plan activation or claim approval respectively.

Payment
02

Non-Payment Removal

If a subscriber fails to pay for 3 consecutive months, their plan is automatically cancelled and they are removed from coverage. A new registration will be required to re-join.

Benefit Tiers
03

Standard vs Premium — Beyond Repair

Standard plans cover repair costs only. If your device is assessed as beyond repair, Guard Your Tech is not liable for replacement under a Standard plan.

Premium plans include beyond-repair cover — if your device cannot be fixed, we will work with you toward a replacement solution.

General Terms All plans are subject to assessment at drop-off. Guard Your Tech reserves the right to decline repairs where damage is assessed as intentional or outside the scope of the selected plan. Coverage begins from the date of first successful payment. Guard Your Tech operates from an office in the Fairgrounds, Gaborone. Our service is open to all university students in Gaborone — you do not need to be a BAC student to register.

Our Story

Protection Born On Campus

We understand student life in Gaborone. A broken device shouldn't derail your academic journey — no matter which university you attend.

Mission

Why We Built Guard Your Tech

We started Guard Your Tech because we saw students losing valuable study time — and money — whenever their devices broke. Repair shops were expensive, far from campus, and not built with students in mind. We also noticed that many students don't back up their work on Google Drive or the cloud — so when a device breaks, they risk losing everything: assignments, projects, and months of hard work.

Guard Your Tech brings the repair service to you. Drop your device off at our office in the Fairgrounds, Gaborone. We collect it, take it for repair, and return it back to you. No wasted trips, no hassle.

Our service is open to all university students in Gaborone — UB, BIUST, Limkokwing, Ba Isago, ABM, and more. You don't have to be a Bac student to register and use Guard Your Tech.
